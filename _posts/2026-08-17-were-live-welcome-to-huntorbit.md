Today HuntOrbit officially goes live. Here's what we're building, and why.

## The problem we started with

Search for a review of almost any SaaS product and you'll find the same thing: ten articles that all read like they were written from the same press release, none of them mentioning what the product actually feels like to use for more than five minutes. That's not useful if you're the one about to spend your team's budget on it.

## What HuntOrbit actually does

Every review published here starts with genuine, hands-on use of the product — the free tier, the onboarding flow, the pricing page, the parts that don't live up to the marketing. We publish a plain star rating, real specifics on pricing and feature limits, and an honest verdict, including when a popular tool doesn't deserve the hype.

We're starting with a handful of categories — productivity tools, CRMs, marketing platforms, and developer tools — and we'll expand from there as we go.

## What's coming next

Over the coming days, we're publishing our first batch of full reviews, one at a time rather than all at once, so each one gets a proper read rather than getting lost in a launch-day pile. If you want to know when something new goes up, our [category pages](/) make it easy to browse by the kind of tool you're actually looking for.

## Want to contribute?

If you've genuinely used a SaaS product and have an honest opinion worth reading, we're actively looking for contributors — writers and SaaS companies alike. Details are on our [Write for Us](/write-for-us/) page.

Thanks for being here on day one.
