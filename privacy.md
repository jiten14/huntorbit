## The short version

HuntOrbit is a content site, not a platform. We don't run user accounts, forms, or a database for this site, so there's very little of yours to collect in the first place. If that ever changes — for example, if we introduce a paid service — the same rule applies: **we do not sell or share your personal data with third parties**, full stop.

## Information we collect

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns — which pages get read, general geographic region, device type, and how people found the site. This data is aggregated and isn't used to identify you individually.

**Payment information (only if applicable).** If HuntOrbit ever offers a paid product or service, we would collect only your name and email address, solely to process that payment and send you a receipt or related confirmation. We don't collect or store payment card details ourselves — that's handled by a licensed payment processor, and we never sell, rent, or share this information with anyone else.

**Everything else.** This site has no comment system, no newsletter signup form, no account creation, and no contact form. If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence like any other email — we don't add you to a marketing list without your explicit consent.

## Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to distinguish sessions and returning visitors. You're free to block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings — the site will continue to work normally either way.

## Third-party links

Reviews on HuntOrbit link out to the SaaS products we cover. Once you click through and leave huntorbit.com, that product's own privacy policy governs what happens to your data — we encourage you to read it, since we have no control over third-party sites.

## Sponsored content

Some reviews on HuntOrbit are sponsored. Sponsorship never affects what data we collect about you as a reader — our data practices are identical whether a review is sponsored or not, and are covered by this same policy.

## Children's privacy

HuntOrbit isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage checking back periodically if you have concerns.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to know what data (if any) we hold about you, can be sent to [connect@huntorbit.com](mailto:connect@huntorbit.com).
