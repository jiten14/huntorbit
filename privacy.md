## Overview

HuntOrbit is a static site — we don't run a database, and we don't collect any information directly through forms, since this site has none.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic patterns (pages visited, general location, device type). This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual visitors.

## Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser to track sessions. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

## Third-party links

Reviews on this site link out to third-party SaaS products. Once you leave HuntOrbit, that product's own privacy policy applies.
