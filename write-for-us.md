## Why write for HuntOrbit

Most "SaaS review" content online is written by someone who spent ten minutes on a product's homepage. We built HuntOrbit around the opposite idea: every review comes from real, hands-on use — the free tier, the onboarding flow, the pricing page, the parts that don't work as well as the marketing says. If you've genuinely used a SaaS product and have an honest opinion worth reading, we want it.

For writers, that means a byline on a review site that stands behind editorial honesty rather than pay-to-play placements. For SaaS companies, it means a review your prospective customers will actually trust, because it isn't the only kind we publish.

## What we publish

- **Hands-on product reviews** — a single SaaS tool, tested and reviewed with real screenshots, specifics, and an honest star rating
- **Head-to-head comparisons** — two tools solving the same problem, compared on the details that actually matter to a buyer
- **Category round-ups** — occasionally, a curated look at the best tools for a specific job

We don't publish sponsored placements disguised as independent opinion, AI-generated filler, or reviews of products the writer hasn't actually used.

## Who this is for

**Independent writers and reviewers.** If you write about SaaS, productivity tools, marketing platforms, or developer tools — and you can back it up with real usage — we're a home for that work.

**SaaS companies and founders.** Want your product reviewed? Submit it through the same form. If it's a genuine fit for our audience, we'll review it like any other submission. We also offer sponsored reviews for companies who want a guaranteed, in-depth write-up — sponsorship is always disclosed, and it buys thoroughness, not a guaranteed positive verdict.

## Our guidelines

- You've used the product yourself for at least a couple of weeks — not a five-minute demo
- The review is specific: real pricing tiers, real feature limits, real trade-offs — not vague praise
- 1,200–2,000 words, written in plain, direct language a non-technical reader can follow
- Original work, not published anywhere else first
- Any affiliation with the product (you work there, you're an investor, you're an affiliate partner) is disclosed upfront — we'll still consider it, we just need to know

## How submission works

1. **Submit your pitch or draft** through the form below — product name, your angle, and either a full draft or a detailed outline
2. **We review it** within a week and reply either way — approval, requested changes, or a pass
3. **If it's approved**, we'll send a checkout link or invoice by email at that point — payment is only ever requested after your article is confirmed to fit our editorial guidelines, never upfront
4. **We publish it**, with your byline and a link back to your own site or portfolio

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you accept guest posts about SaaS products?**
Yes — that's the core of what HuntOrbit publishes. A hands-on SaaS review or comparison is exactly what we're looking for.

**Is there a fee to submit?**
No. Submitting a pitch or draft is always free. If your article is approved, we'll discuss payment by email at that point — there's no charge just to pitch or to have your work considered.

**Can I write about a product I work for or am affiliated with?**
Yes, as long as you disclose the affiliation in your submission. We'll typically ask you to disclose it in the published article too.

**How long until I hear back?**
Within a week of submitting, one way or another.

**Do you accept sponsored reviews from SaaS companies?**
Yes. Submit through the same form and note that you're interested in a sponsored review — we'll follow up with details. Sponsored reviews are always disclosed as such and held to the same editorial honesty as everything else we publish.
