## What we publish

Practical, hands-on reviews of SaaS products — real usage, real screenshots, honest pros and cons. We don't publish rewritten marketing copy or reviews of products you haven't actually used.

## Guidelines

- You must have used the product yourself for at least a few weeks
- Include specific details — pricing tiers, feature limits, real use cases
- 1,200–2,000 words, written in plain, direct language
- Original content only — not published elsewhere first

## Sponsored reviews

We also accept sponsored reviews from SaaS companies who want an in-depth, honest write-up of their own product. Reach out via the form below for current rates and details.

## How to submit

Fill out the short pitch form below with your proposed topic and a bit about your experience with the product. We reply to every submission within a week.
